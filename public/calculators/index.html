How these work

Built the same way as the app.

Same principle as everything else at Zinfai: your numbers are yours, and a figure that is an estimate should say so.

🔒

Nothing is sent anywhere

Every calculation runs in your browser. There is no request carrying your salary, your rent or your net worth to a server, because there is no server involved.

💱

Eight currencies

Switch between INR, USD, GBP, EUR, AED, AUD, SGD and CAD. Rupee amounts group in lakh and crore, the way you would actually write them.

📋

Assumptions on the page

Each calculator states what its model does and what it ignores — tax, fees, sequence risk — so you can judge how much weight the answer deserves.

📊

Every chart has a table

Behind each chart is the same data as rows and columns, so nothing is locked behind a hover, and the numbers are yours to copy.
