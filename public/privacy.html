Privacy

We can't see your money.

Not as a policy choice — as an architectural one. Zinfai runs on your machine with no server behind it. We hold an email address so you can download it, and nothing else.

Last updated: 2 October 2026

The short version

Zinfai is software you install and run yourself. Your holdings, transactions, expenses, income, liabilities, goals and plans are stored in a database on your own computer. We operate no backend that receives them, so we could not read your financial data even if we wanted to.

This website, www.zinfai.com, does have one account system: downloading an installer requires a free account with a verified email address. That account is for the download only — it holds your email, never your finances, and the app you install never signs in to it. There are still no tracking or analytics scripts on this site.

What stays on your machine

  • Every asset, account, transaction, expense, income and liability you record.
  • Your local login credentials, stored as a hash in your own database.
  • Uploaded statements, and everything extracted from them.
  • AI-generated plans, ratings and analyses, once returned.
  • Your AI provider API keys, in your local database or a local .env file.
  • Daily portfolio snapshots and the whole history they build up.

What leaves your machine, and when

Zinfai reaches out to the internet for a handful of specific things. Each one is listed here so there are no surprises.

  • Market prices and FX rates. The app requests quotes for the instruments you hold from public market-data sources (exchange quote APIs, AMFI for mutual fund NAVs, public crypto APIs) and daily foreign-exchange rates. These requests contain ticker symbols and scheme codes — never your quantities, cost basis or identity.
  • Market, macro and news data. Index levels, commodity prices, macro series and news feeds for your region. Nothing about you is sent.
  • AI provider requests — only if you configure a key. When you generate a financial plan, rate or value an asset, parse an unsupported statement or tax document, categorise expenses, run news analysis or ask Mr Mirror a question, the relevant data is sent from your machine directly to the provider you chose (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, Mistral, DeepSeek, Groq, Perplexity, OpenRouter, AWS Bedrock, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint you configure) using your own API key. It does not pass through us. That data is handled under that provider's privacy terms, so read them if this matters to you. Mr Mirror in particular sends the portfolio figures it looked up to answer your question — that is how it answers from your own numbers — and never your passwords, statement passwords or API keys. Its web searches send only the search text. Leave the keys blank and no AI request is ever made.
  • Google Drive backups — only if you enable them. The backup file is encrypted on your computer with a passphrase only you know, then written to your own Drive account, with access you grant and can revoke at any time. We never receive it, and neither Google nor anyone else can read it without the passphrase. See Google user data below.
  • Update checks. The app checks for a newer release so it can show the update banner. This is a plain request for a version number and carries no data about you — it is not tied to your download account.

Google user data

Two Zinfai apps can connect to your Google account: Zinfai on your computer, to write backups to Drive, and Zinfai Buddy on your phone, to read them. Both ask Google for the same single Drive permission, drive.file, together with your email address so each app can show which account is connected. drive.file lets an app see only the files Zinfai itself created. Neither app can open, list or read anything else in your Drive.

  • What is accessed: the backup folder Zinfai creates, the backup files it writes there and the dated copies in its History folder, and your Google account email address.
  • How it is used: Zinfai uploads your backup, lists your backups and downloads one when you ask to restore it. Zinfai Buddy lists your backups and downloads the latest one to show your portfolio on the phone. Buddy never writes, changes or deletes anything in your Drive. The email address is shown on screen and used for nothing else.
  • Encryption: backups are encrypted before they leave your computer. Buddy decrypts them on the phone with the passphrase you enter there. The passphrase is never sent anywhere.
  • Where it is kept: Zinfai stores the sign-in token Google issues in the Zinfai database on your own computer. On the phone, the Google sign-in session is held by the operating system's Google sign-in service. Neither is ever sent to us or anyone else.
  • Sharing: none. No Google user data reaches a Zinfai server, an advertiser or an AI provider, and none is used to train any AI model.
  • Removal: click Disconnect on Zinfai's Backup and Recovery page, which also revokes the token at Google; tap Disconnect under Google Drive in Buddy's Settings; or remove Zinfai at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which disconnects both apps. Deleting the backup folder in Drive removes the backups themselves.

Zinfai's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we collect

From the app: nothing. No telemetry, no crash reporting, no analytics, and no way for your financial data to reach us.

From this website, if you register for a download, we hold exactly this:

  • Your email address, and your name if you gave one.
  • How you signed in — email and password, or Google.
  • Whether your email is verified, and when you last signed in.
  • Which installers you downloaded — product, platform, version and time.
  • The IP address and browser the registration and each download came from, kept as an anti-abuse record.

That is the complete list. We do not track which pages you browse, we set no advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not sell, rent or share any of it. The one cookie-equivalent in play is the sign-in token your browser stores to keep you logged in, which you clear by signing out.

Why we ask. So we can tell you when a security or data-correctness update ships, and so we know roughly who is running Zinfai. We do not run marketing campaigns off this list.

Who processes it. Sign-in and the account record are handled by Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) on our behalf. Verification email is sent through our email provider. The installer itself is served from GitHub, which logs the request under its own privacy policy. Our hosting and DNS providers process standard request logs (IP address, user agent, page requested) to serve and protect the site, as any website's do.

How long we keep it. For as long as you have an account. Ask us to delete it and we remove the account and its download history.

If you email us

Writing to support@zinfai.com means we hold your email address and whatever you put in the message, for as long as it takes to help you. We don't add you to any list, and we don't share it.

Please never send passwords, API keys, full account numbers or an unredacted backup file. We will never ask for them.

Zinfai Buddy (Android and iOS)

Zinfai Buddy is the companion app for your phone. It shows the portfolio from your Zinfai backup and cannot change it. Like Zinfai, it has no server behind it: we receive nothing from it.

  • Where your data comes from: the latest Zinfai backup in your Google Drive (see Google user data above), or a backup file you pick yourself from the phone's storage.
  • What stays on the phone: the opened backup, kept in the app's private storage, optionally behind a fingerprint or face lock. Your backup passphrase is kept in the phone's secure keystore (Android Keystore or the iOS Keychain). Alerts and the daily digest are worked out on the phone and shown as local notifications. There are no push notifications and no server sends them.
  • What leaves the phone: requests for live prices, exchange rates, market sentiment and news. These go to public sources: Yahoo Finance, AMFI, CoinGecko, Binance, Coinbase, Gate.io, Frankfurter, ExchangeRate-API, alternative.me, CNN, and the RSS feeds of the news sites shown in the app. Price requests contain only ticker symbols. They never contain your quantities, values or identity. Like any website, those sources see the phone's IP address. A background task repeats the price requests now and then to keep the daily digest current. Apart from Google Drive, nothing else is contacted.
  • What we collect: nothing. Buddy has no account with us, and no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Removal: Clear Cached Data in Buddy's Settings deletes the backup, the saved passphrase and every cached figure from the phone. Uninstalling the app does the same.

Security is yours to hold

Running on your own machine means the safeguards are yours too. Keep your computer and disk encrypted, use a strong password for the app, and export backups regularly — if you lose the machine or delete its data volume, the data is gone and we have no copy to restore from.

By default the app listens on localhost only, so it isn't reachable from your network. If you deliberately expose it, put it behind authentication and HTTPS — that configuration, and its consequences, are yours.

Your data, your call

  • Export — one JSON file with everything, any time, from Settings → Backup.
  • Delete — remove the data volume and the data is permanently gone.
  • Revoke — remove API keys in Settings, or revoke Drive access in your Google account.

None of that needs a request to us, because we hold none of it. For the download account we do hold, email support@zinfai.com and we will send you a copy of the record or delete it, whichever you ask for. Deleting it does not affect a copy of Zinfai you have already installed.
