Privacy

We can't see your money.

Not as a policy choice — as an architectural one. Zinfai has no server, no accounts and no analytics. There is nowhere for your data to go.

Last updated: 11 August 2026

The short version

Zinfai is software you install and run yourself. Your holdings, transactions, expenses, income, liabilities, goals and plans are stored in a database on your own computer. We operate no backend that receives them, so we could not read your financial data even if we wanted to.

This website, www.zinfai.com, is a set of static pages. It has no login, no forms and no tracking scripts.

What stays on your machine

  • Every asset, account, transaction, expense, income and liability you record.
  • Your local login credentials, stored as a hash in your own database.
  • Uploaded statements, and everything extracted from them.
  • AI-generated plans, ratings and analyses, once returned.
  • Your AI provider API keys, in your local database or a local .env file.
  • Daily portfolio snapshots and the whole history they build up.

What leaves your machine, and when

Zinfai reaches out to the internet for a handful of specific things. Each one is listed here so there are no surprises.

  • Market prices and FX rates. The app requests quotes for the instruments you hold from public market-data sources (exchange quote APIs, AMFI for mutual fund NAVs, public crypto APIs) and daily foreign-exchange rates. These requests contain ticker symbols and scheme codes — never your quantities, cost basis or identity.
  • Market, macro and news data. Index levels, commodity prices, macro series and news feeds for your region. Nothing about you is sent.
  • AI provider requests — only if you configure a key. When you generate a financial plan, rate an asset, parse an unsupported statement or run news analysis, the relevant data is sent from your machine directly to the provider you chose (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, DeepSeek or Mistral) using your own API key. It does not pass through us. That data is handled under that provider's privacy terms, so read them if this matters to you. Leave the keys blank and no AI request is ever made.
  • Google Drive backups — only if you enable them. The backup file is written to your own Drive account, with access you grant and can revoke at any time. We never receive it.
  • Update checks. The app checks the public downloads repository for a newer release so it can show the update banner. This is a plain request for a version number and carries no data about you.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account system, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no analytics and no cookies set by this website. We do not build a profile of you, because we have nothing to build one from.

Two ordinary exceptions apply, and they're the same ones any website has. Our hosting provider and DNS provider process standard request logs (IP address, user agent, page requested) to serve and protect the site. And if you download an installer, that request goes to GitHub, which logs it under its own privacy policy.

If you email us

Writing to support@zinfai.com means we hold your email address and whatever you put in the message, for as long as it takes to help you. We don't add you to any list, and we don't share it.

Please never send passwords, API keys, full account numbers or an unredacted backup file. We will never ask for them.

Zinfai Buddy

The Android app is read-only and has no account. The backup you import is kept in the app's private storage on the device, optionally behind a biometric lock, and can be erased with one tap in Settings. Its only outbound calls are to the same public market-data sources for live prices and news, and to Google Drive if you sync that way. There are no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in it.

Security is yours to hold

Running on your own machine means the safeguards are yours too. Keep your computer and disk encrypted, use a strong password for the app, and export backups regularly — if you lose the machine or delete the Docker volume, the data is gone and we have no copy to restore from.

By default the app listens on localhost only, so it isn't reachable from your network. If you deliberately expose it, put it behind authentication and HTTPS — that configuration, and its consequences, are yours.

Your data, your call

  • Export — one JSON file with everything, any time, from Settings → Backup.
  • Delete — remove the Docker volume and the data is permanently gone.
  • Revoke — remove API keys in Settings, or revoke Drive access in your Google account.

There is no request to file with us for any of this, because we hold none of it.

Not financial advice

Zinfai is a tracking and planning tool for your own research and education. The AI planner and asset quality ratings are AI-generated and can be wrong. Zinfai is not a registered investment adviser, broker or tax adviser, and nothing in the app or on this site is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Verify anything that matters, and consider a registered adviser in your own jurisdiction.
