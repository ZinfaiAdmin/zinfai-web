Track

Portfolio Management

One ledger for everything you own and owe — in the currency you own it in, kept current without you typing prices.

40+ asset types, across five countries.

Equities and foreign-listed stocks, ESOPs and RSUs, mutual funds, ETFs, gold and silver, sovereign gold bonds, crypto, fixed and recurring deposits, PPF, EPF, NPS, gratuity and pension, NSC, KVP, SCSS, SSY, MIS, corporate and RBI bonds, land, house property, REITs and InvITs, insurance, physical cash and collectibles — plus the jurisdiction-specific instruments each country actually uses: 401(k), IRA, Roth IRA, HSA, 529, ISAs, SIPPs, gilts, Premium Bonds, superannuation, sukuk and more.

  • Multi-account — bank, demat and crypto accounts with independent cash balances.
  • Multi-portfolio — self, spouse, retirement or children, each with its own base currency and location.
  • Custom attributes — tag holdings, bank balances and demat cash "long-term", "emergency fund", "high risk" — one at a time or many at once — then analyse by tag.
  • Full transaction history — buys, sells, dividends, interest and fees, powering XIRR.

Multi-currency engine

An ISO 4217 currency on every asset and transaction, native amounts preserved, live FX for current value and frozen cost FX for basis — so profit and loss shows the currency move instead of hiding it.

📄

Smart statement processing

Upload a file and Zinfai parses it — bank and credit-card statements, broker holdings and tradebooks, password-protected NSDL/CDSL CAS files, and EPF, PPF, NPS and SSY statements. Anything without a built-in parser falls through to AI.

Automated price updates

Prices fetched per exchange in the instrument's own currency — NSE/BSE, US markets, LSE, ASX, AMFI NAVs, crypto and MCX — every 30 minutes, with daily FX revaluation.

A dashboard you build yourself

Pick cards from every part of the app — net worth, daily P&L, allocation, performance, income, expenses, budgets, maturities, equity vs index, attribute and asset insight — then drag and resize them into place. Add the same card twice with different settings; every card remembers its own. One click resets to the default.

🏢

Corporate actions, gifts & transfers

Record splits, bonuses, rights issues, demergers, IPO allotments and symbol, name or ISIN changes as transactions, so capital gains and XIRR follow automatically. Gift or move shares between demat accounts, singly or in bulk, carrying the original cost and purchase dates with them.

🏦

Net worth & liabilities

First-class home, car, personal and gold loans, overdrafts and BNPL, with automatic principal-interest splits, amortization schedules and payoff progress. Net worth = assets + cash − liabilities.

📸

Daily snapshots & daily P&L

A snapshot of your whole portfolio at a day-close time you choose, in your own time zone. Each day's P&L is what your holdings gained since the snapshot before — money you added or took out is not counted as a gain. Missed days are filled in at each day's own prices.

🏧

Close out to your bank

When a PF, PPF, gratuity, deposit, bond or any of 24 schemes matures or is closed, pay it into the right bank account, net of TDS, in one step. Record PF partial withdrawals without closing the account. The later bank statement line is recognised, so the money isn't counted twice.

🧮

Expense tracking

Extract expenses from bank and credit-card statements, categorised by AI. Each row is routed as expense, income, investment or uncategorised, so credits and SIPs aren't counted as spending. Group and amortize one-off costs across a window.

💰

Income & investment tracking

20 income categories from salary to cashback, one-time or recurring, plus SIP, lumpsum, recurring and periodic investments — auto-synced from statements and linked back to the source transaction.

⚙️

SIP / STP / SWP utilities

Set up systematic investment, transfer and withdrawal plans, and retroactively generate historical SIP transactions at the right NAV so your XIRR is accurate from day one.

When there's no built-in parser, AI reads it instead.

The built-in parsers are exact and free — they know precisely where each column sits. The AI parser is the safety net for everything else, and it is deliberately not India-specific: it works out the statement's own currency, date format and decimal separator from the document rather than assuming a country.

  • Any country, any currency — ISO 4217 inferred from symbols, IBAN, bank name or language, with per-row currency for forex lines.
  • Any language or script — descriptions are preserved verbatim, never translated, with an optional English merchant name alongside.
  • Cross-checked — the statement goes to every AI provider you've configured, and Zinfai keeps the result the providers most agree on.
  • Direction verified — debit versus credit is confirmed against the running balance, not guessed from wording.
  • Learns each layout — after one successful AI read, Zinfai saves the layout and checks it against the same file. From then on that bank's or form's statements are read locally, at no cost.

This is the one feature that leaves your machine. The AI parser only runs if you have supplied your own API key, and it sends the statement's text to the provider you chose. Leave the keys blank and Zinfai still reads every institution listed above — you simply lose the fallback. Full detail in the privacy statement.

Income Tax · India

Your tax year, worked out from what you already own.

Zinfai already knows your salary credits, interest, dividends, rent and every sale. The Income Tax module puts them under the heads the Act uses, works out the tax under both regimes, and checks the result against the department's own documents.

Five pages, one financial year at a time.

  • Profile — PAN, date of birth, residential status and regime; your Chapter VI-A deductions (80C, 80D, 80CCD and the rest); losses brought forward; and the figures only a document can give, such as gross salary from Form 16.
  • Returns — upload the Intimation u/s 143(1) or the ITR-V and Zinfai reads the income, the tax and whether you have a demand or a refund. It checks the form's own arithmetic too.
  • Profit & Loss — income by head, and capital gains worked out lot by lot. Each gain is short- or long-term under the right section (111A, 112A, 112, 115BBH), and every sale is listed. A sale with no recorded buy is flagged with a button to add one.
  • Computation — old and new regime side by side, with every deduction named by its section. Exempt income such as PPF and EPF interest is shown separately rather than silently dropped.
  • Calendar — advance-tax instalments and filing deadlines, as reminders in your alerts inbox.
🧾

Only actual figures

Nothing is projected to the end of the year. The advance-tax schedule uses the income received so far, and says so when that makes an early instalment look low.

📌

Every figure shows its source

A figure from the filed return, your Form 16 or your bank credits says which one it came from. Where the app can't tell something from your data, it asks you rather than guessing.

The Income Tax module is a record-keeping and estimation tool for Indian resident individuals, not tax advice. File your return from your own documents, and consult a chartered accountant where your situation calls for one.

Not just tracking — a plan to reach your goals. Zinfai's robo-advisor turns your complete financial picture into a personalized, goal-based plan: what to invest, where, and how much each month to actually get there.

Set your goals. Get a plan built around them.

The planner assembles a full financial snapshot and asks the AI provider of your choice to generate a plan tailored to you. Retire

Financial Freedom — the FIRE engine

Go beyond net-worth tracking and answer the real questions: when can I stop working, how much is enough, and will the money last?

A year-by-year plan, not a single number.

Zinfai computes your Lean, Regular, Fat and Coast FIRE targets from your age, expenses, savings, monthly investment, expected returns and inflation — then projects net worth, contributions, withdrawals and portfolio balance for every year until, and through, retirement.

  • Income sources — salary, bonus, rental and pension streams with start and end dates and growth rates.
  • Milestones & debts — education, a home purchase, and active EMIs with interest and tenure.
  • Bucket strategy — short, medium and long-term buckets with rebalancing rules and per-instrument constraints.
  • Withdrawal simulator — test withdrawal sequences against market scenarios, with resiliency stress tests and an optimizer.
  • Tax rules — slabs, deductions and regime selection feed straight into net withdrawals.
  • Scenarios & sensitivity — save "what if" variants and compare them side by side.
Insights

Understand the portfolio, and the market it lives in.

Dedicated analysis surfaces that go past "what is it worth" to "how good is it, what's maturing, and what is the market doing".

Asset Quality — know how good each holding really is.

Zinfai puts your AI provider to work as a research analyst, scoring every stock and mutual fund you own from 1.0 to 10.0 with the reasoning to back it up. Scores are cached, refreshed daily by a scheduler, and can be re-run on demand.

  • Stocks — competitive moat, financial health, valuation, growth and management, weighed into one score.
  • Mutual funds — rolling returns vs benchmark, Sharpe and alpha, portfolio construction, expense ratio and manager record.
  • 3Y / 5Y / 10Y verdicts — a clear Buy, Hold or Sell call for each horizon, each with its thesis.
  • Peers & best-in-class — five direct peers ranked, the best-in-class name called out, and who the asset actually suits.
💎

Intrinsic value

An intrinsic value for every stock you hold, blending four models (discounted cash flow, earnings power, Graham, and shareholder payouts) into one figure with a margin of safety and a verdict. It runs on the company's reported per-share figures, each dated to its fiscal period, and a methodology page shows exactly how it's calculated.

Mr Mirror

Ask about your money. Get an answer from your own numbers.

Mr Mirror is a chat assistant available on every screen. Ask it "how much did I earn in dividends this year?", "which of my funds overlap?" or "what's maturing next quarter?", and it answers from your own records rather than from general knowledge.

🪞

Answers from your data

It has 32 ways to look things up across the whole app: holdings, returns, spending, budgets, SIPs, alerts, ratings, rates, the market calendar, your FIRE details and your financial plan. Figures about you come only from those records, and it says when an explanation is general knowledge instead.

📊

Tables and charts when they help

It answers briefly: tables for figures, bullet points for comparisons, a chart when the shape of the numbers matters. Links take you straight to the relevant screen.

🌐

The web, when your data is silent

For questions about the market rather than your portfolio, it can run a web search through a provider that supports search. It never presents an answer from memory as a live lookup.

🔒

Look, don't touch

Asking a question never changes your data. Retirement and planning questions are handed to the FIRE and Planner modules that own those numbers, so the chat never contradicts your own screens.

Bring your own AI key — or use none at all.

Every AI feature in Zinfai runs through a key you supply, for the provider you choose: OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, xAI Grok, Mistral AI, DeepSeek, Groq, Perplexity, OpenRouter or AWS Bedrock — and anything else that speaks the OpenAI API works too, via a custom endpoint. Plans, ratings, statement parsing, Mr Mirror's answers and news analysis are generated with your key and stored in your local database. There is no Zinfai server in the middle — because there is no Zinfai server.

Leave the keys blank and everything else still works: tracking, statements in supported formats, prices, XIRR, snapshots, net worth, alerts and the entire FIRE engine are all deterministic and need no AI at all.

No key yet? Google Gemini, Groq and OpenRouter all offer free keys. On first login Zinfai shows you how to get one in a couple of clicks and tests it before saving.
