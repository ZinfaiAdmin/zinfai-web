When there's no built-in parser, AI reads it instead.

The built-in parsers are exact and free — they know precisely where each column sits. The AI parser is the safety net for everything else, and it is deliberately not India-specific: it works out the statement's own currency, date format and decimal separator from the document rather than assuming a country.

  • Any country, any currency — ISO 4217 inferred from symbols, IBAN, bank name or language, with per-row currency for forex lines.
  • Any language or script — descriptions are preserved verbatim, never translated, with an optional English merchant name alongside.
  • Cross-checked — the statement goes to every AI provider you've configured, and Zinfai keeps the result the providers most agree on.
  • Direction verified — debit versus credit is confirmed against the running balance, not guessed from wording.

This is the one feature that leaves your machine. The AI parser only runs if you have supplied your own API key, and it sends the statement's text to the provider you chose. Leave the keys blank and Zinfai still reads every institution listed above — you simply lose the fallback. Full detail in the privacy statement.

Plan

The AI Financial Planner

Not just tracking — a plan to reach your goals. Zinfai's robo-advisor turns your complete financial picture into a personalized, goal-based plan: what to invest, where, and how much each month to actually get there.

Set your goals. Get a plan built around them.

Tell Zinfai about yourself once — age, income, expenses, risk appetite, tax slab, insurance, liabilities, and the goals that matter (a child's education, a home, early retirement). The planner assembles a full financial snapshot and asks the AI provider of your choice to generate a plan tailored to you. Review and edit the exact prompt before anything is sent, or paste back a response you ran elsewhere. Every plan is versioned with the exact input snapshot, so you can compare and accept one later.

Retire

Financial Freedom — the FIRE engine

Go beyond net-worth tracking and answer the real questions: when can I stop working, how much is enough, and will the money last?

A year-by-year plan, not a single number.

Zinfai computes your Lean, Regular, Fat and Coast FIRE targets from your age, expenses, savings, monthly investment, expected returns and inflation — then projects net worth, contributions, withdrawals and portfolio balance for every year until, and through, retirement.

  • Income sources — salary, bonus, rental and pension streams with start and end dates and growth rates.
  • Milestones & debts — education, a home purchase, and active EMIs with interest and tenure.
  • Bucket strategy — short, medium and long-term buckets with rebalancing rules and per-instrument constraints.
  • Withdrawal simulator — test withdrawal sequences against market scenarios, with resiliency stress tests and an optimizer.
  • Tax rules — slabs, deductions and regime selection feed straight into net withdrawals.
  • Scenarios & sensitivity — save "what if" variants and compare them side by side.
Insights

Understand the portfolio, and the market it lives in.

Dedicated analysis surfaces that go past "what is it worth" to "how good is it, what's maturing, and what is the market doing".

Asset Quality — know how good each holding really is.

Zinfai puts your AI provider to work as a research analyst, scoring every stock and mutual fund you own from 1.0 to 10.0 with the reasoning to back it up. Scores are cached, refreshed daily by a scheduler, and can be re-run on demand.

  • Stocks — competitive moat, financial health, valuation, growth and management, weighed into one score.
  • Mutual funds — rolling returns vs benchmark, Sharpe and alpha, portfolio construction, expense ratio and manager record.
  • 3Y / 5Y / 10Y verdicts — a clear Buy, Hold or Sell call for each horizon, each with its thesis.
  • Peers & best-in-class — five direct peers ranked, the best-in-class name called out, and who the asset actually suits.

Bring your own AI key — or use none at all.

Every AI feature in Zinfai runs through a key you supply, for the provider you choose: OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, xAI Grok, Mistral AI, DeepSeek, Groq, Perplexity, OpenRouter or AWS Bedrock — and anything else that speaks the OpenAI API works too, via a custom endpoint. Plans, ratings, statement parsing and news analysis are generated with your key and stored in your local database. There is no Zinfai server in the middle — because there is no Zinfai server.

Leave the keys blank and everything else still works: tracking, statements in supported formats, prices, XIRR, snapshots, net worth, alerts and the entire FIRE engine are all deterministic and need no AI at all.
