Multi-currency by design

For investors whose money no longer lives in one currency.

Zinfai keeps each account in its natural currency while normalizing portfolio overviews into the base currency you choose. Track INR holdings, India-based USD brokers such as Vested and INDmoney, UK bank accounts, US retirement accounts, UAE savings products and Australian superannuation — without mixing symbols, markets or reporting currencies.

Nothing is added together until it shares a currency.

You pick a base currency and base location once; each portfolio can override them, so a household can run an INR portfolio and a GBP one side by side. Every asset and transaction stores its own ISO 4217 currency alongside the native amount you actually entered — and those native figures are never overwritten when values are recomputed.

Conversion is honest about time, too: the FX rate behind your cost basis is frozen at purchase, while current value is revalued daily at the live rate. Base-currency profit and loss therefore includes the currency movement rather than quietly hiding it.

Jurisdiction catalogue

The instruments each country actually uses.

A 401(k) isn't a PPF and an ISA isn't a mutual fund. Zinfai ships a versioned catalogue of jurisdiction-specific instruments, each locked to its mandated currency, with contribution caps and lock-in periods surfaced as dated informational metadata rather than hard blocks.

How it works

Up and running in minutes — on any OS.

Run the Mac or Windows installer. It sets up everything — database, backend and UI — on your machine.
